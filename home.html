ABOUT TAU GAMMA PHI

The fraternity Tau Gamma Phi is based on the concepts of brotherhood, service, leadership, and community. Its members work to achieve self-improvement through these values while also making a positive contribution to the community.

OUR CHAPTER

Tau Gamma Phi – Conception Chapter is a society of Triskelions who uphold brotherhood, service, and self-improvement. Our chapter fosters great camaraderie within our ranks as we engage in various activities geared towards helping the community.

OUR MISSION

The purpose of our organization is to foster true brotherhood, nurture responsible leaders, and serve our community through action. Our aim is to provide an atmosphere where members will be able to grow, cooperate, and contribute positively to society.
